⚖ Public Records

Official, public-by-law sources for your own safety awareness · ← back to search
Read this first — it matters.
  • This is not a formal background check. Screening a tenant or employee is regulated by the FCRA and needs a proper, disclosed, compliant service. This is for your personal safety awareness, not formal rent/hire decisions.
  • One input, not a verdict. Common names muddy results, not everything is online, a clean record isn't proof someone's safe, and an old minor record isn't proof someone's dangerous.
  • Trust your gut too. If someone gives you weird vibes, that instinct flagged something before any search did. Records are a second opinion on it — not a replacement.

Safety registries

National Sex Offender Public Website (NSOPW)
Run by the U.S. Dept. of Justice. Searches every state registry at once by name or location. The go-to for "this date/roommate gives me weird vibes."

More court records

PACER
The official U.S. federal court records system (dockets + filings). Authoritative; small per-page fee.
Your state's court portal
State criminal/civil case search varies. Search "[your state] judiciary case search" or "[county] court records" — most are free and official. (The live search above covers published *opinions*, not every state docket.)

Who they say they are

Professional license lookup
Verify a nurse, contractor, therapist, agent, etc. is really licensed: search "[state] [profession] license verification" — every state board has an official lookup.
News mentions
Run their name + town in Sovereign Search — local news is public and often the most human signal.
What this deliberately does NOT do: no data brokers (Spokeo, BeenVerified, TruthFinder, Instant Checkmate, Whitepages-premium) — those sell aggregated, often-wrong dossiers, and that's surveillance-for-profit, not public records. No dark web, ever. Only official, public-by-law sources.
