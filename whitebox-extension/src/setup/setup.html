Welcome

WhiteBox gives you portable user memory for AI agents. Your memory lives as plain markdown files on your disk that any AI — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini — can read and write to. No vendor lock-in, no cloud syncing, no account required.

This wizard will walk you through five quick steps. Should take about three minutes. Skip whatever you don't want to fill in — you can edit everything later in any text editor.

Nothing leaves your machine. The browser asks for permission to read/write one folder you choose; that's the only thing that ever happens.

Pick a folder for your vault

WhiteBox needs one folder on your disk to be your vault — where your identity, preferences, and observations get stored. You can pick an empty folder; we'll set it up for you.

Suggested location: ~/whitebox-vault (or anywhere in your Documents folder). Avoid synced cloud folders if you can — local-only is the safest default.

You're ready

Vault is set up, identity and working style written, agents will pick them up automatically. Try it now:

  1. Open claude.ai/new in a new tab.
  2. Send any message. The first one of a fresh chat will silently include your vault context.
  3. Ask Claude "what do you actually know about me from the vault?" — it should answer with what you just wrote.
